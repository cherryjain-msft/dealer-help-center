# Fees and charges

## Cancellation and no-show fees

There are no cancellation, late-cancellation or no-show fees. Diagnostic and repair pricing isn't affected.

## Diagnostic fees

Some services start with a diagnostic inspection. Your service advisor tells you about any diagnostic fee and gets your approval before work begins. Diagnostic fees are separate from the cost of repairs.

## Questions about a charge

Contact your dealer's service advisor and have your appointment details ready.
