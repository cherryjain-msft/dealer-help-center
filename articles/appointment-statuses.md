# What your appointment status means

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Booked | Your appointment is scheduled. |
| Checked in | The dealer has checked in your vehicle. Key-drop vehicles are checked in when the service department opens. |
| In service | A technician is working on your vehicle. |
| Ready for pickup | The work is finished and your vehicle is ready for pickup. |
| Completed | You picked up your vehicle. |
| Canceled | You or the dealer canceled the appointment. |
| No-show | You didn't arrive and didn't cancel. |

**Completed**, **Canceled** and **No-show** appointments stay visible under **Past and canceled** for 90 days after the appointment start time. All other statuses appear under **Upcoming**.

Only **Booked** appointments can be canceled online, with at least 2 hours until the start time, including the exact 2-hour deadline. If you can't cancel online, contact your dealer or service advisor. See [Cancel or change a service appointment](cancel-or-change-an-appointment.md).
