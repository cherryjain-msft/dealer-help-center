# What your appointment status means

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Your appointment is booked. |
| Checked in | The dealer has your vehicle and keys. |
| In service | A technician is working on your vehicle. |
| Ready for pickup | Your vehicle is ready to collect. |
| Completed | Your service is finished. |

Canceled appointments are removed from your appointment list.

Need to cancel? See [Cancel or change a service appointment](cancel-or-change-an-appointment.md).
