# Drop off your vehicle and check in

## At the service desk

Arrive a few minutes early. Your service advisor checks you in, confirms the work and gives you an estimated pickup time.

## After-hours key drop

Many dealers have a secure key drop for early or overnight drop-off.

1. Park in a marked service space.
2. Fill out a key envelope with your name, phone number and appointment time.
3. Seal your key in the envelope and put it in the key drop.

Key-drop vehicles are checked in when the service department opens.

## After check-in

Once your appointment is **Checked in**, you can't cancel it online. Contact your service advisor if your plans change.
