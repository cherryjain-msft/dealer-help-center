# Cancel or change a service appointment

## Cancel online

You can cancel a service appointment in the customer portal only when its status is **Booked** and there are **at least 2 hours** until the start time. You can cancel at the exact 2-hour deadline, too. The portal shows the exact time until which you can cancel online.

1. Sign in to the customer portal and open **My service appointments**.
2. Find the appointment under **Upcoming**.
3. Select **Cancel appointment**.

After cancellation, the appointment shows **Canceled** under **Past and canceled** for 90 days after its start time.

## When you can't cancel online

If there are less than 2 hours until your appointment starts, call your dealer. If the status is no longer **Booked**, contact your dealer or service advisor.

Once your vehicle is **Checked in**, including a key-drop vehicle, contact your service advisor instead of canceling online. Key-drop vehicles are checked in when the service department opens.

There are no cancellation or no-show fees. Diagnostic and repair pricing isn't affected. See [Fees and charges](fees-and-charges.md).

## Change the date or time

Online rescheduling isn't available yet. If your appointment is eligible for online cancellation, cancel it and book a new one. Otherwise, contact your dealer or service advisor to discuss changing the date or time. See [Book a service appointment](book-an-appointment.md).
