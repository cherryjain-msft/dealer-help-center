# Cancel or change a service appointment

## Cancel online

You can cancel a service appointment in the customer portal up to **48 hours** before the start time.

1. Sign in to the customer portal and open **My service appointments**.
2. Find the appointment under **Upcoming**.
3. Select **Cancel appointment**.

## Less than 48 hours before your appointment

Call your dealer to cancel. If you miss your appointment without canceling, a no-show fee may apply. See [Fees and charges](fees-and-charges.md).

## Change the date or time

Online rescheduling isn't available yet. To change the date or time, cancel your appointment and book a new one. See [Book a service appointment](book-an-appointment.md).
