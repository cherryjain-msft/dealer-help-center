# Service appointments FAQ

## How late can I cancel online?

You can cancel online up to 2 hours before your appointment starts, as long as it isn't checked in yet. After that, call your dealer.

## Is there a fee to cancel or for missing an appointment?

No. There are no cancellation or no-show fees.

## Can I reschedule online?

Not yet. Cancel the appointment and book a new one. See [Book a service appointment](book-an-appointment.md).

## Where do canceled appointments go?

They move to **Past and canceled** in the customer portal and stay there for 90 days.
