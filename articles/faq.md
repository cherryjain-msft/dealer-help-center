# Service appointments FAQ

## How late can I cancel online?

You can cancel online only when your appointment is **Booked** and there are at least 2 hours until it starts, including the exact 2-hour deadline. The portal shows the exact time until which you can cancel online. With less than 2 hours remaining, call your dealer. If the appointment is no longer **Booked**, contact your dealer or service advisor. For **Checked in** appointments, including key-drop vehicles, contact your service advisor.

## Is there a fee to cancel or for missing an appointment?

No. There are no cancellation or no-show fees.

## Can I reschedule online?

Not yet. If your appointment is eligible for online cancellation, cancel it and book a new one. Otherwise, contact your dealer or service advisor to discuss changing the date or time. See [Book a service appointment](book-an-appointment.md).

## Where do canceled appointments go?

They show **Canceled** under **Past and canceled** in the customer portal. **Completed**, **Canceled** and **No-show** appointments stay there for 90 days after the appointment start time. See [What your appointment status means](appointment-statuses.md).
