# Book a service appointment

Book by phone with your dealer or on your dealer's website. You can't book in the customer portal yet.

Have your vehicle's year, model and mileage ready, plus a short description of any issue.

After you book, the appointment appears in the customer portal under **Upcoming** with the status **Booked**.

Before your visit, read [Drop off your vehicle and check in](drop-off-and-check-in.md).
